Software developer employment in the United States

BLS OEWS national estimates · May 2006 – May 2025 · counts jobs, not people or postings

Each point is the number of jobs held by software developers in the United States in May of that year: 801,580 in 2006, 1,687,890 in 2025 — an increase of 886,310 (110.6%). The numbers come from the Bureau of Labor Statistics’ Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics (OEWS) program, which surveys employers rather than households.

What it counts: jobs, not people. The survey covers part-time and full-time workers paid a wage or salary, so one person holding two covered jobs counts twice, and the self-employed, owners and partners in unincorporated firms, household workers and unpaid family workers are not counted at all. Each value is a May snapshot, not an annual average, and it is not job postings or industry payroll totals.

How the data was sourced

  • One point per May survey, read from that year’s OEWS national estimates workbook (national_M{YYYY}_dl.xls / .xlsx) in BLS’s oesm{YY}nat.zip, using the TOT_EMP column for whichever software-developer occupation code(s) BLS published that year.
  • Where the occupation was split across two codes (2006–2018), employment is the sum of the two rows. 2019–2020 used the combined code 15-1256; 2021 on uses 15-1252.
  • The current release file was taken from BLS directly. The older zip files were retrieved from the Internet Archive’s raw snapshots of the same BLS URLs, because bls.gov returns 403 to this machine’s IP range.
  • Checks run before publishing: all 20 years re-read from the raw workbooks match the charted series exactly; May 2018 also matches BLS’s published static national table (903,160 applications + 405,330 systems software = 1,308,490); the latest release on BLS’s list is May 2025, which is the 1,687,890 shown here.
  • Caveats: OEWS estimates are not revised after release. Employment for software developers fell from 1,476,800 in 2020 to 1,364,180 in 2021, but part of that drop is definitional — the 2019–2020 numbers include QA analysts and testers, who moved to their own code (15-1253) in 2021.
