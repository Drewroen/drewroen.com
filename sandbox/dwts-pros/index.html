Dancing with the Stars: the professional dancers

35 seasons · 437 celebrity–professional couples · 54 professionals · 2005 – 2026

Every season since 2005 the show pairs a celebrity with a professional dancer, and the professional leaves with whatever the celebrity earned. This page counts what happened to the professionals rather than the celebrities: 437 couples across 35 seasons, 424 distinct celebrities and 54 professional partners. Nineteen of those professionals have won at least one Mirrorball Trophy; the 34 completed seasons contain exactly 34 wins, so every trophy is accounted for.

What counts as one competition: a season in which the professional was paired with a celebrity. That is not the same as appearing on the show — the dance troupe has always contained professionals who were never given a celebrity partner, and they are not counted here (see the caveats at the bottom). A partnership that withdrew mid-season counts as a season competed but is excluded from every average, because it has no finishing position. Season 35 is airing as this was written: its 13 surviving couples are listed but excluded from win rates and averages.

How the data was sourced

  • The base record is Wikipedia’s List of Dancing with the Stars (American TV series) competitors, which lists one row per celebrity–professional partnership with the season and finishing position. Its wikitext was parsed directly, not read by eye: 437 rows across four tables, with rowspan cells expanded so tied eliminations and multi-season columns resolve correctly.
  • Every professional’s name is taken from the sortname template rather than the rendered text, so stage names do not split a career in two. Two professionals married and changed how they are billed mid-career, and both spellings were merged into one record: Ashly DelGrosso (one season billed Ashly Costa) and Kym Johnson (one season billed Kym Herjavec).
  • Checks run before publishing: the parsed season lists for all 54 professionals were diffed against the separate professional cast-timeline table on the show’s main article — 0 differences across 35 seasons, including which seasons ended in a win. The winners were then diffed against the professional_winner field in each of the 34 completed season articles — 0 differences. The cast size of every season was checked against that season’s own contestant table — all 35 match. Wins sum to 34, exactly one per completed season.
  • An outside check on the headline numbers: press tallies of the show’s winning professionals also count 19 winners with Derek Hough on six, which is what this page computes.
  • Caveats, all of which change what the numbers mean rather than the numbers themselves. First, troupe-only professionals are excluded: a dancer who performed on the show for years without a celebrity partner has no season here. Second, Dancing with the Stars: Juniors (2018) is a separate show with its own professionals and is not counted. Third, finishing position is not a measure of dancing: it is the result of the judges’ scores combined with the public vote, and the show has changed how those combine more than once, so early-season finishes are not strictly comparable with late ones. Fourth, a professional’s record is heavily conditioned on the celebrity they were handed, which is why a low win rate over many seasons is a statement about casting as much as about ability.
  • Season 35 is incomplete, so its 13 surviving couples carry no finishing position and are excluded from every average and rate above; only the 3 couples already eliminated that season are counted.
